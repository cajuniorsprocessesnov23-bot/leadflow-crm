Workflow Rules

LeadFlow calculates the next reminder from each lead’s stage and latest call outcome. Log every call so the next task stays accurate.

Verification calls

Unverified leads receive call attempts according to the verification schedule. Mark whether the contact answered, confirmed interest, or is not a valid lead.

Sales follow-ups

Verified leads receive scheduled follow-ups. Update the stage when a group is created, work is processed, completed, or dropped.

Recovery and client care

Dropped work gets recovery reminders. Completed clients receive weekly client-care reminders, and birthdays due within seven days appear on the dashboard.

Reminders are calculated when the CRM is opened. Automated WhatsApp messages and background notifications are not enabled.
