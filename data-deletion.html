Legal Wegal – How to delete your data

You can ask Legal Wegal to delete the personal information we hold about you, including your WhatsApp messages with us.

Stop WhatsApp messages

Reply STOP to our WhatsApp number (+91 93700 77727). We stop sending you WhatsApp messages straight away.

Request deletion

  1. Email legalwegal.com@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or send "DELETE MY DATA" to our WhatsApp number.
  2. Include your name and the phone number you used with us.
  3. We confirm your identity by replying to that phone number or email.

We delete your contact details, call notes, documents, portal details and WhatsApp message history within 30 days and confirm by email or WhatsApp. If we must keep some records by law (for example tax invoices and payment records), we keep only those, for the period the law requires, and delete them afterwards.
