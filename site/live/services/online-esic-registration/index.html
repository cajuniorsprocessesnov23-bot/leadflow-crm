ESIC Registration Online in India

Employee State Insurance (ESI) provides medical and cash benefits to employees. Employers covered by the ESI Act must register and contribute monthly. We register your establishment and set up monthly filings.

Who needs ESIC Registration?

  • Establishments with 10 or more employees in notified areas
  • Businesses with employees earning within the ESI wage limit

Documents required

  • Business registration proof
  • PAN and address proof
  • Employee details
  • Bank details

ESIC Registration – frequently asked questions

What is the wage limit?

Employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month are generally covered.

What are the contribution rates?

Currently 3.25% by the employer and 0.75% by the employee.

Is it mandatory?

Yes, for covered establishments.
