Annual ROC Compliance Filing – frequently asked questions

What are the main due dates?

Typically AOC-4 within 30 days and MGT-7 within 60 days of the AGM for companies; LLP Form 11 by 30 May and Form 8 by 30 October.

What is DIR-3 KYC?

Annual KYC for every director with a DIN.

Can late filings be made?

Yes, with additional fees.
