Annual compliance checklist for a Private Limited Company

The yearly compliance every Private Limited Company in India must complete: board meetings, AGM, audit, ROC forms AOC-4 and MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC and ITR.

Registering a company is the start. To stay in good standing, every Private Limited Company must complete these filings every year, even with zero turnover.

Within the year

  • Board meetings: at least four a year, with a gap of not more than 120 days between two meetings (small companies and OPCs have relaxations).
  • Statutory registers and minutes: kept up to date.
  • Auditor: the first auditor is appointed within 30 days of incorporation.

After the financial year ends (31 March)

  • Statutory audit of the accounts.
  • Annual General Meeting (AGM) by 30 September.
  • AOC-4: financial statements, within 30 days of the AGM.
  • MGT-7 or MGT-7A: annual return, within 60 days of the AGM.
  • Income tax return: by the due date for companies.
  • DIR-3 KYC: every director, by 30 September.

Penalties

ROC forms filed late attract an additional fee of ₹100 per day per form, with no upper cap. Long delays can lead to the company being struck off and directors being disqualified.
