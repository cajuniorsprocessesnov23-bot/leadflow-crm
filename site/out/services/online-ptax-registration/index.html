Professional Tax Registration – frequently asked questions

Is professional tax charged in every state?

No, only in states that levy it.

What is PTRC vs PTEC?

PTRC is for deducting from employees; PTEC is the business's own enrolment (Maharashtra).

Is there a maximum amount?

Professional tax is capped at ₹2,500 per year per person.
