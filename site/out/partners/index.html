Home › Partner program
For CAs, accountants, consultants & agents

Partner with Legal Wegal and earn on every client you refer

Refer clients or resell our services under your own billing. We do the filing; you keep the relationship and get paid automatically.

  • Commission is calculated on our professional fee and becomes payable as soon as your client pays
  • Live status of every client in your partner portal
  • Your own referral link and code
  • Monthly payouts to UPI or bank with TDS certificate (2% u/s 194H above ₹20,000 a year)
  • Reseller option with partner prices

Questions first?

By submitting you agree to be contacted by call / WhatsApp about your request.

How it works

  1. Sign up with your mobile number below. We verify your details.
  2. Get your code (e.g. LWP-0001) and referral link.
  3. Refer or add clients — share your link or add them in the portal.
  4. Earn — commission is created automatically when the client pays, and paid out monthly.

Partner portal

Partner login / sign up

We send a 6-digit code to your WhatsApp.

Register as a partner

An admin reviews your details, usually within one working day, and gives you a partner code.

Partner terms (summary)

When is commission earned?

When a client tagged to you pays. It is calculated on our professional fee only, not on government fees, and reversed if the payment is refunded.

Who can be tagged to me?

New clients who enquire through your link or code, or whom you add in the portal. Existing Legal Wegal clients cannot be re-tagged.

Tax deduction

TDS under section 194H is deducted at 2% once your commission passes ₹20,000 in a financial year, or 20% if PAN is not provided.

Conduct

Do not promise government outcomes or timelines, and do not use Legal Wegal's name in paid ads without written approval. Accounts can be suspended for misuse.
