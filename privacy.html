Legal Wegal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Legal Wegal ("we", "us"), a legal and compliance services business in India (legalwegal.com), collects, uses and protects personal information when you enquire about or use our services, including when we contact you on WhatsApp.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, city and company name.
  • Service details: the services you ask about (for example GST, company registration, trademark, labour law), notes from our calls with you, and the status of your work.
  • Documents and access details you choose to share so we can do your work, such as documents, government portal links and login details.
  • Payment records: invoices, amounts paid and pending, and payment dates. We do not store card or bank account numbers.
  • WhatsApp messages: messages and button replies you send to our official WhatsApp number (+91 93700 77727), and the delivery status of messages we send you.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and call you back.
  • To send you service updates on WhatsApp, such as call-back confirmations, work progress, document dispatch and tracking details, payment reminders and receipts.
  • To do the work you hired us for and keep records required by law.
  • To manage our team's tasks internally (for example, which staff member is handling your file).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising by third parties.

3. WhatsApp messages and opting out

We send WhatsApp messages using the official WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. You can stop WhatsApp messages from us at any time by replying STOP, or by tapping Not interested where offered. Reply START to turn them back on. Opting out does not affect any work already in progress; we may still contact you by phone or email about it.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run our business, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) – to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Supabase – secure cloud database that stores our client records.
  • Cloudflare – hosting for our internal client-management system.
  • Google – Google Drive, where client documents are stored.
  • Government departments and portals, only as needed to complete the work you asked for, and authorities where required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep client records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements (generally up to 8 years after the work ends). Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted or anonymised within 24 months of the last contact, or sooner if you ask.

6. Security

Access to client records is limited to authorised staff with individual logins and role-based permissions. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop messaging you. See how to request deletion. We reply within 30 days.
